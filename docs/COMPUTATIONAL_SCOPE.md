# Computational scope and proof boundary

`zqverify` provides exact finite-group algebra, exact integer cubical incidence, deterministic extraction checks, exhaustive finite detector searches over a declared catalogue, and exact tiny-volume Wilson calculations.

It does **not** establish the general theorems by computation. In particular, no finite test suite proves volume-uniform source-sector domination, the exact projected filling formula for every rectangle, the infinite history-tree majorant, or passage to thermodynamic Gibbs limits. Those statements are proved in the manuscript.
